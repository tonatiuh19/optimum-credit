QUICK GLOSSARY (read this first)
HINT: Pipeline = the steps a job/deal/case moves through (like columns on a board: Lead -> In progress -> Done).
HINT: CRM = your shared list of people + history (who they are, notes, messages, status) — not a personal phone or spreadsheet.
HINT: Prospect / lead = someone interested but not a paying customer yet.
HINT: Contact (address book) = someone you text/call who is NOT in the sales pipeline (realtor, vendor, event meet).
HINT: Portal = a private web login for clients or partners to upload docs, see status, or pay — without emailing files around.
HINT: Phase 1 = the first release we build (must-haves). Phase 2 = later upgrades.
HINT: Soft-warn vs hard-block = soft-warn says "this phone already exists" but lets you save; hard-block refuses duplicates.
HINT: Magic link = login link sent by email/SMS (no password to remember).
